Help & Support

Welcome to My Shop Help Center. Here you can find answers to common questions and learn how to use our website.

🛒 How to Place an Order?

1. Go to the Home page.
2. Click on a product.
3. Click Order Now.
4. Your order will be saved and visible in the Orders page.

📦 Where Can I See My Orders?

Click on the Orders option in the navigation bar. All your placed orders will be shown there.

❤️ What is Add to Cart?

Add to Cart lets you save products temporarily before ordering them.

🔐 Is Login Mandatory?

No. Login is optional. You can browse products and place orders without logging in.

❌ Can I Cancel an Order?

Currently, order cancellation is not available. This feature may be added in future updates.

⚠️ Facing Issues?

If something is not working properly, try refreshing the page. If the problem continues, visit the Contact page to reach us.

Still need help? Contact our support team
